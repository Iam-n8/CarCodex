🏡 Home Equipment, HVAC & Maintenance Items

TrustedParts.com Inventory API: Sponsored by the Electronic Components Industry Association (ECIA) 
to promote authorized component tracking. Access is completely free for commercial apps and provides 
generous request limits to cross-reference component models, electronic parts, and internal 
maintenance materials.

Open-Meteo API: While not a hardware database, this is an essential free 
maintenance tool used in predictive commercial property apps. It requires no authentication and 
provides historical and live localized weather data, allowing your software to mathematically 
predict residential HVAC runtime strain, filter replacement intervals, and seasonal equipment degradation.
